About FoodShare

FoodShare is a food donation initiative that connects food donors with needy people. Our mission is to reduce food waste and fight hunger in our community.

What We Do

🍱 Food Collection

We collect surplus food from homes, restaurants and events.

🤝 Food Distribution

We distribute collected food to people in need and local communities.

❤️ Reduce Food Waste

We encourage people to donate extra food instead of throwing it away.
